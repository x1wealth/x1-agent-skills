THE QUILL FAMILY REVOCABLE TRUST
Dated March 4, 2019

ARTICLE ONE. ESTABLISHMENT OF TRUST

1.1 Declaration. Elena Quill and Marcus Quill, of Portland, Oregon, as settlors (together, the "Settlors"), declare that they hold the property described on Schedule A, and any property later transferred to the trustee, in trust on the terms of this agreement.

1.2 Name. This trust shall be known as the Quill Family Revocable Trust dated March 4, 2019.

1.3 Revocability. While both Settlors are living, either Settlor may revoke this trust in whole or in part as to community property, and each Settlor may revoke it as to that Settlor's separate property, by a signed writing delivered to the trustee.

1.4 Family. The Settlors have two children, Rosalind Quill Park and Owen Quill.

ARTICLE TWO. TRUSTEES

2.1 Initial Trustees. The initial trustees are Elena Quill and Marcus Quill. Either initial trustee may act alone on behalf of the trust.

2.2 Successor Trustee. If both initial trustees cease to serve, Rosalind Quill Park shall serve as successor trustee. If Rosalind Quill Park is unable or unwilling to serve, First Cascade Trust Company shall serve as successor trustee.

2.3 Incapacity. A trustee shall be considered unable to serve upon written certification by two licensed physicians that the trustee cannot manage his or her financial affairs.

ARTICLE THREE. ADMINISTRATION DURING THE SETTLORS' LIVES

3.1 Income and Principal. During the Settlors' joint lives, the trustee shall pay to or apply for the benefit of the Settlors as much of the net income and principal as either Settlor directs.

3.2 Tax Treatment. The Settlors intend this trust to be a grantor trust for federal income tax purposes during their lives.

ARTICLE FOUR. DISTRIBUTION AFTER THE DEATH OF BOTH SETTLORS

4.1 Division. After the death of the surviving Settlor, the trustee shall divide the remaining trust property into equal shares, one for each child of the Settlors then living.

4.2 Distribution to Children. Each child's share shall be distributed outright to that child upon reaching age thirty (30).

ARTICLE FIVE. GENERAL PROVISIONS

5.1 Governing Law. This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Oregon.

5.2 Additions. Any person may add property to this trust by will, beneficiary designation, deed, assignment, or other transfer acceptable to the trustee.

Signed on March 4, 2019.

Elena Quill, Settlor and Trustee
Marcus Quill, Settlor and Trustee
