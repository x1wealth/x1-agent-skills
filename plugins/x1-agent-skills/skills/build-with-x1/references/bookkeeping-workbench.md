# Bookkeeping evidence workbench

Help a household owner, bookkeeper or fractional CFO prepare the evidence and questions for a specific entity and period. Preserve their checklist, row IDs, categories and accounting workflow. If the checklist isn't supplied, offer a neutral example without claiming it represents their bookkeeping requirements.

## Use it

“Here’s my checklist for this entity and month. Fill in what the supplied records establish, keep my row IDs, and give me the questions that still need an answer.”

Keep document presence, entity/period match and accounting status separate. A bank statement doesn't establish reconciliation. An invoice doesn't establish payment. A document for another entity can't complete the row. An unsuccessful search describes that query, not every place a document might exist.

Use the source's wording for any actual reconciliation status and say which entity, account and period it covers. Don't treat a prepared question or a draft checklist tick as an accounting entry.

## Make it mine

“Turn this into an editable workbench in this chat. Keep my rows. Let me group by entity or month and see the evidence beside each question. I want to change the layout myself.”

Keep editable presentation and private working notes separate from sourced facts. Don't invent a live accounting integration. On refresh, retain row identity and notes, show the changed evidence, and leave unresolved accounting status unresolved. Use the current host's supported private output; qualify storage and the artifact's own connection before retaining client records there.

## Extend my project

“Add evidence and question columns to this checklist in my existing app. Keep the current schema and row IDs. Use my authorized X1 reads where they remove manual searching.”

Map returned source references into the existing rows instead of replacing the checklist or copying the whole household graph. For professional work, resolve the selected client and permitted documents on each relevant request. One client relationship doesn't permit another. A missing read capability is a specific dependency, not a reason to use a broad operator token.

## A useful second visit

A new statement may resolve the missing-statement question while reconciliation remains open. A new reconciliation report may resolve only the stated account and period. Keep unrelated rows and user-authored notes intact; don't rebuild the whole checklist. Prepare questions such as “Can you share the reconciliation evidence for this account and period?” without sending them.

Check correct entity/month, wrong entity/right month, right entity/wrong month, incomplete selected-file coverage, duplicate evidence and explicit reconciliation evidence with a narrower scope. Avoid transaction posting, categorization, tax filing or marking a period closed unless the user separately requests an available operation under its actual execution contract.
